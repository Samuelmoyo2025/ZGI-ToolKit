Zinara Levy Summary Tool

Upload the Zinara workbook — one sheet per office. Every row across every sheet is combined (columns D–H plus J–N), producing a Complete Summary, a Source Tracking log, and a full Detailed Summary. No row is merged away, even duplicate registration numbers.

Source workbook

Each sheet should hold rows with: Office (col B), Registration Number (col C), then NWP / Stamp Duty / Gvt Levy / Zinara / Radio in columns D–H, repeated again in columns J–N.

Sheets named "Complete Summary", "Source Tracking" or "Detailed Summary" are skipped automatically (they're treated as prior output, not source data) — so it's safe to re-run this on a workbook you've already summarised before.
