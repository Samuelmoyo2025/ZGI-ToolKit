Data-quality exceptions

Definitions. Loss Ratio = −Gross Claims Incurred ÷ GWP (written basis). Commission Ratio = −Net Commission ÷ GWP; a negative value means net commission income. Combined Ratio = Loss + Commission (claims and commission only — management expenses are not in the export, so this understates true combined). Growth = YTD vs Last-Year YTD GWP. All figures are year-to-date. Dashboard. Book ratios are premium-weighted (total claims or commission ÷ total GWP), so they differ from a simple average of broker ratios. Retention = NWP ÷ GWP. In the movement chart a broker is "new" with no GWP last year and "lost" with none this year. Attention flags apply to brokers with GWP ≥ 50,000: loss-making (combined ≥ 100%), buying growth (growth ≥ 25% at ≥ 100% combined), deteriorating (combined ≥ 15 pts worse than last year), high commission (≥ 5 pts above the book rate), losing volume (premium down ≥ 20% while profitable).
Thresholds. Tier A needs GWP ≥ 50,000 and combined < 75%. Ratio and growth league tables are limited to brokers with GWP ≥ 50,000 (growth also needs prior-year GWP ≥ 25,000) so tiny-base blow-ups don't dominate.
