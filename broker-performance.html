Data-quality exceptions

Definitions. Loss Ratio = −Gross Claims Incurred ÷ GWP (written basis). Commission Ratio = −Net Commission ÷ GWP; a negative value means net commission income. Combined Ratio = Loss + Commission (claims and commission only — management expenses are not in the export, so this understates true combined). Growth = YTD vs Last-Year YTD GWP. All figures are year-to-date.
Thresholds. Tier A needs GWP ≥ 50,000 and combined < 75%. Ratio and growth league tables are limited to brokers with GWP ≥ 50,000 (growth also needs prior-year GWP ≥ 25,000) so tiny-base blow-ups don't dominate.
